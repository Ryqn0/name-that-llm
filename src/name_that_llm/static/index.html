Which LLM wrote this?

Paste a passage written by an AI chatbot. The app guesses which of 18 models wrote it.
Most likely
⚠ Before you trust this
  • It is not an AI detector. It only knows these 18 models and always picks one of them, even for text written by a human or by another AI.
  • It learned from 2,699 passages from a Kaggle competition (about 150 per model), so other topics or writing styles can fool it.
  • On new passages, it names the exact model right about 4 times in 10.
  • Your text is not stored.
